01 / CONCEPT

A Different Vehicle Interface.

NeuroCar studies whether trained non-invasive EEG patterns serve as one additional intent input channel for human mobility. The system does not attempt to read thoughts, scan emotions, or decode continuous speech.

Instead, it recognizes a tightly constrained vocabulary of trained neural execution patterns. Every prediction passes through an independent Guardian safety layer before simulated or physical execution.

"Intent is treated as a request for action, not an imperative command."
